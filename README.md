# WaitWatch

**Risk-adapted detection and clinical response to deterioration in emergency department waiting areas.**
Team Early Bird · Scope-AI-Thon 2026, McGill University

### ▶ [Open the live demos](https://mozhou-vicky.github.io/Wait-Watch/)

Patients can deteriorate between nursing checks while they wait in the ED. WaitWatch combines nurse assessments, medical records and continuous monitoring, with signal-quality checks and a named nurse responsible for every alert. AI helps prioritise who to watch most closely; transparent clinical rules raise the alarm; clinicians make every decision.

## Demos

Open in any browser, no install needed.
